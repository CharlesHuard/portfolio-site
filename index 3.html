Professional Standards

1. Value and Care for All Students

"I will always strive to create an environment that inspires trust, respect, and excitement for learning for all."

2. Role Models Acting Ethically

"I am committed to constantly improving, learning from experiences, and adapting my approach to meet the needs of my students."

3. Understanding Student Growth

"With awareness of the individual strengths and challenges of each student, I will create a class that upholds the feeling of belonging."

4. Involving Parents and Community

"I will work with parents to develop learning skills and outcomes with students, engaging the local community wherever possible."

5. Effective Practices

"I believe in creating interactive and fun learning experiences based on constructive principles where students build their base of knowledge."

6. Broad Knowledge Base

"I believe in the critical role of core competencies in guiding meaningful learning experiences, in line with the seven OECD learning principles."

7. Career-Long Learning

"My path as an educator is steeped in a commitment for continuous improvement and flexibility."

8. Contributing to the Profession

"I will actively cultivate strong relationships with my colleagues, regularly engaging in collaborative work that will build up my skills as an educator."

9. Respect for Indigenous Histories

"I require myself to incorporate First Peoples Learning Principles into my teachings of the curriculum, using resources from organizations like the First Nations Education Steering Committee."
