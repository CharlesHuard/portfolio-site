Professional Standards

1. Value and Care for All Students

I prioritize creating an inclusive, student-centered learning environment. I demonstrate care by building strong, respectful relationships and ensuring emotional and physical safety. My lessons are designed to value diversity and foster belonging for all students.

2. Role Models Acting Ethically

I conduct myself with integrity, both inside and outside the classroom. I uphold the dignity of the teaching profession and model ethical behavior to students, understanding the importance of trust and credibility in my role as an educator.

3. Understanding Student Growth

I apply knowledge of child development, individual learning needs, and social-emotional growth to inform my teaching decisions. I adapt instruction and assessment to support diverse learners and create opportunities for every student to succeed.

4. Involving Parents and Community

I actively communicate with parents and guardians, valuing their input and fostering partnerships that support student learning. I seek ways to engage with the local community, recognizing its role in enriching students’ educational experiences.

5. Effective Practices

I use a range of planning, instructional, and assessment strategies to facilitate student learning. I reflect on and refine my practices, ensuring they are responsive to student needs and aligned with curricular goals and competencies.

6. Broad Knowledge Base

I bring a strong foundation of subject knowledge and pedagogical understanding, integrating curriculum content with meaningful, culturally relevant learning experiences. I communicate effectively and guide students to explore curricula in a Canadian and global context.

7. Career-Long Learning

I engage in professional learning and reflective practice, continually developing my teaching philosophy and skills. I identify my professional needs and actively pursue opportunities for growth, collaboration, and improvement.

8. Contributing to the Profession

I support and collaborate with colleagues, contributing to school and district initiatives. I share ideas, mentor peers, and engage with professional communities to strengthen collective practice and enhance student learning.

9. Respect for Indigenous Histories

I honor the histories, cultures, and contributions of First Nations, Inuit, and Métis peoples. I integrate Indigenous worldviews into my teaching, reflect critically on my own biases, and contribute to truth, reconciliation, and healing in education.
